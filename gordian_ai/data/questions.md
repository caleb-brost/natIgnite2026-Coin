# Capture-A-Win Question Pool

Each section below lists candidate questions for the Gordian AI capture flow.
The AI is expected to ask some questions per section but may pick, adapt,
or reorder based on the user's win type and prior answers. The first question
in each section is the "anchor" — start there. Treat the rest as a menu, not
a checklist. Combine or rephrase when an answer already covers a topic.

---

## Strategy

What was the main strategic goal or purpose behind this win?
Which company vision, mission, value, or priority did this win advance?
What problem or opportunity were you addressing — for whom?
Why did this win matter to the broader organization or market?
What alternative strategies did you weigh and decline, and why?
What signals told you the timing was right to pursue this?
What constraint shaped the strategy more than anything else?

## Work Plan

What was the practical plan that helped make this win happen?
How did you sequence the major workstreams or milestones?
What tasks had to be completed, and which were the long poles?
What resources — time, budget, approvals, tools — were required?
Where did the plan need to flex when reality diverged?
What dependency or hand-off was the highest-risk part of the plan?
What was the go/no-go criterion that kept the plan honest?

## People

Who helped make this win happen, and in what role?
What skills, attitudes, or communication strengths were decisive?
Who needed to be Accountable, who Consulted, and who just Told?
Where did interpersonal friction or alignment most influence the outcome?
What did you ask of someone that stretched them, and how did they respond?
Whose contribution is least visible in the result but mattered most?

## Operations

What systems, processes, tools, or policies supported this win?
What operational barriers had to be solved, and how?
Which departments or functions were involved, and how did they coordinate?
What would need to be standing for someone else to repeat this win?
Where did existing playbooks or runbooks help, and where did they fail?
What manual or hidden work made the result possible?

## Results

What measurable results showed this was a win?
What quantitative outcomes can you cite — revenue, cost, time, throughput, quality?
What qualitative outcomes — trust, morale, customer voice, brand?
What is the repeatable lesson — the one rule worth keeping?
What surprised you about the result, positive or negative?
If you ran this again, what would you do differently to compound the gain?
